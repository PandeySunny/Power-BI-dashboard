# Credit Card Transaction & Customer Analytics Project  
**Mastercard – Data Analyst Internship**

---

## Project Overview
This project was completed as part of my **Data Analyst Internship at Mastercard** and focuses on analyzing credit card transaction data and customer demographics to generate actionable business insights. The project uses interactive Power BI dashboards to evaluate revenue performance, spending behavior, customer segmentation, card usage trends, and seasonal patterns. The dashboards are designed to support executive-level decision-making and targeted marketing strategies.

---

## Project Objectives
- Analyze transaction volume, revenue, and interest across multiple dimensions  
- Identify high-value customer segments based on demographics and income  
- Understand card usage behavior and transaction channels  
- Track seasonal and quarterly revenue trends  
- Provide clear, interactive dashboards for business stakeholders  

---

# 1. Credit Card Transaction Report

## Description
This dashboard highlights transaction volumes, expenditure categories, revenue distribution, and card usage trends.

---

## KPIs
- **Revenue:** $55M  
- **Interest Earned:** $7.84M  
- **Transaction Amount:** $45M  
- **Transaction Count:** 656K  

These KPIs summarize overall card activity and transactional performance.

---

## Revenue Breakdown

### By Expenditure Type
- Bills generate the highest revenue (**$14M**)  
- Entertainment follows with **$10M**  
- Travel and Food contribute the least

### By Education Level
- Graduates are the top contributors (**$22M**)  
- Post-graduates contribute **$3M**  
- Doctorate holders contribute **$2M**

### By Job Category
- Businessmen dominate spending (**$17M**)  
- White-collar workers contribute **$10M**  
- Retirees generate the least revenue (**$5M**)

### By Card Category
- **Blue cards** dominate with **$46M revenue**  
- Silver, Gold, and Platinum cards significantly underperform

### By Transaction Channel (Chip Usage)
- Swipe transactions: **$35M revenue**  
- Chip transactions: **$17M revenue**  
- Online transactions: **$3M revenue**

---

## Revenue & Transaction Trends
- **Q3** records the highest revenue (**$14.2M**) and transaction volume (**166.6K**)  
- **Q1** follows closely with **$14M revenue**  
- **Q2** shows the second-highest transaction volume (**164.2K**)

# 2. Credit Card Customer Report

## Description
This dashboard focuses on customer demographics, income, spending habits, and revenue contribution across multiple attributes.

---

## KPIs
- **Total Revenue:** $55M  
- **Total Interest:** $7.84M  
- **Total Income:** $576M  
- **Customer Satisfaction Score:** 3.19  

These KPIs summarize business performance and customer experience.

---

## Revenue Breakdown

### By Income Group
- High-income customers generate **$22M** (highest)  
- Medium-income customers generate **$8M**  
- Low-income customers generate **$10M**

### By Age Group
- Age group **40–50** contributes the most (**$25M**)  
  - Females: $11M  
  - Males: $14M  
- Younger (20–30) and senior (60+) groups contribute less

### By Marital Status
- Married customers generate **$28M**  
- Single customers generate **$23M**  
- Unknown marital status group is negligible

### By Dependent Count
- Families with **3 dependents** generate the highest revenue (**$16M**)  
- Households with no dependents show lower spending

### By Education Level
- Graduates dominate with **$22M revenue**  
- Doctorate groups contribute the least

### By Weekly Trends
- Revenue spikes observed:
  - February, May, September (Males)
  - March, August (Females)

### By State
Top revenue-generating states:
- **Texas (TX):** $13M (Females $6M, Males $7M)  
- **New York (NY):** $13M (Females $6M, Males $7M)  
- **California (CA):** $13M (Females $7M, Males $6M)

---

## Customer Job Analysis
This table displays **Revenue, Interest Earned, and Income by Job Category**:
- Businessmen: $186M income, $25M interest (Top contributors)  
- White-Collar: $103M income, $14M interest  
- Government: $88M income, $11M interest  
- Self-employed: $75M income, $11M interest  
- Blue-Collar and Retirees generate the least revenue

---

# Insights & Business Story

- High-income and educated customers are the most valuable segments  
- Bills and Entertainment are the top spending categories  
- Blue cards dominate performance due to higher adoption and usage  
- Customers aged 40–50 show the highest spending due to financial stability  
- Revenue peaks in **Q3 and Q1**, showing strong seasonal patterns  
- Males consistently spend more than females across all demographics  
- The largest gender gaps are seen in:
  - Age 40–50 group ($14M males vs $11M females)
  - Married customers ($15M males vs $13M females)
- High-performing states like TX and NY show a consistent **$1M higher male spending**, presenting targeted marketing opportunities
