# Credit Card Transaction & Customer Analysis Project

## Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive analysis of credit card transactions and customer demographics using interactive dashboards. It highlights transaction volumes, revenue distribution, customer behavior, card usage trends, and demographic insights to support data-driven business and marketing decisions.

---

# 1. Credit Card Transaction Report

## Description
This report highlights transaction volumes, categories, revenue distribution, and card usage trends.

---

## KPIs (Revenue, Interest, Transaction Amount, Transaction Count)

### Top KPIs
- **Revenue:** $55M  
- **Interest:** $7.84M  
- **Transaction Amount:** $45M  
- **Transaction Count:** 656K  

These KPIs summarize overall transactional data and card activity.

---

## Revenue Breakdown

### By Expenditure Type
- Highest revenue is from **Bills ($14M)** and **Entertainment ($10M)**  
- Travel and Food categories contribute the least

### By Education Level
- Graduates contribute the most revenue **($22M)**  
- Post-graduate and Doctorate groups contribute **$3M** and **$2M** respectively

### By Job
- Businessmen (**$17M**) and White-Collar workers (**$10M**) dominate spending  
- Retirees contribute the least (**$5M**)

### By Card Category
- **Blue cards** generate the highest revenue (**$46M**)  
- Silver, Gold, and Platinum cards lag significantly

### By Chip Usage
- Swiping accounts for **$35M revenue**  
- Chip transactions generate **$17M**  
- Online transactions contribute the least (**$3M**)

---

## Revenue and Total Transaction Count
- **Q3** sees the highest revenue (**$14.2M**) and transaction volume (**166.6K**)  
- **Q1** follows closely with **$14M revenue**  
- **Q2** records **164.2K transactions**, making it the second-highest by volume

# 2. Credit Card Customer Report

## Description
This report focuses on customer demographics, spending habits, and revenue contribution across different attributes.

---

## KPIs (Revenue, Total Interest, Income, Customer Satisfaction Score)

### KPIs
- **Revenue:** $55M  
- **Total Interest:** $7.84M  
- **Income:** $576M  
- **Customer Satisfaction Score:** 3.19  

These KPIs summarize business performance and customer satisfaction levels.

---

## Revenue Breakdown

### By Income Group
- High-income group contributes the most (**$22M**)  
- Medium-income group contributes **$8M**  
- Low-income group contributes **$10M**

### By Age Group
- Age group **40–50** contributes the most (**$25M**)  
  - Females: $11M  
  - Males: $14M  
- Younger (20–30) and senior (60+) groups contribute less

### By Marital Status
- Married customers contribute **$28M**  
  - Females: $13M  
  - Males: $15M  
- Single customers contribute **$23M**  
- Unknown marital status group is insignificant

### By Dependent Count
- Households with **3 dependents** generate the highest revenue (**$16M**)  
  - Females: $7M  
  - Males: $9M  
- Families with no dependents show lower spending

### By Education Level
- Graduates dominate revenue with **$22M**  
- Doctorate groups contribute the least

### By Week
- Revenue spikes:
  - February, May, September (Males)
  - March, August (Females, slightly lower than males)

### By State
Top revenue-generating states:
- **Texas (TX):** $13M (Females $6M, Males $7M)
- **New York (NY):** $13M (Females $6M, Males $7M)
- **California (CA):** $13M (Females $7M, Males $6M)

# Insights and Story from the Data

- High-income and educated customers are the most valuable revenue contributors  
- Customers spend the most on Bills and Entertainment, indicating practical and lifestyle-driven spending  
- Blue cards dominate performance, likely due to wider adoption or better benefits  
- The **40–50 age group** is the most profitable, reflecting financial stability  
- Revenue peaks in **Q3 and Q1**, showing strong seasonal spending patterns  
- Males consistently outperform females in spending across all categories and demographics  
- The most significant gender gap is observed in:
  - Age group 40–50 ($14M males vs $11M females)
  - Married customers ($15M males vs $13M females)
- High-performing states (TX and NY) show a consistent **$1M higher spend by males**, reinforcing targeted marketing opportunities
